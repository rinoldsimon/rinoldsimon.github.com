About

Still building. Still curious.

I’m a Senior Full-Stack Engineer based in Chennai, with ten years of shipping web apps and distributed systems. Rails and React are home base. These days I’m also leaning into Python and FastAPI — using tools like Cursor, Claude, and Gemini to write cleaner code and move through hard bugs faster.

Most of my recent work has been with remote US teams: student systems, hiring products, platform migrations. I’m also building EliteCoders, a product studio. When I’m away from the keyboard I’m at the gym, and a proud dad to two kids.

I write about Rails on my tech blog, and I hang around Stack Overflow — about 3,000 reputation, mostly Rails and the old Ember.js days.
